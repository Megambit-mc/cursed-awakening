# Changelog

## 1.7.0 — The Cursed Update

*Released November 9, 2026 · CurseForge.*

### ✨ New
- **Swarms grow with your grade:** Fly Heads, Cursed Moths, Crawling Curses, cockroaches, squids, centipedes, Grade 4 Cursed Spirits and Floating Eyes come alone or in small groups for beginners, and in swarms of up to 12-16 for Grade 1 and Special Grade sorcerers.
- **Four canon curses that were missing:** the **Eishu Cursed Womb** (it sits still and beats louder and louder; if you don't exorcise it within about 3 minutes it matures into a **Finger Bearer**, now a low **Special Grade** curse as in the anime), **Ubusunagami** (the land god from Hidden Inventory), **Geto's Semi-Grade 1 Curse** (Jujutsu Kaisen 0; Geto NPCs now always carry it) and the **Head Necklace Curse** (a fat grey giant with one green eye on top of its head and a necklace of heads).
- **Heavenly Restriction (energy) rework:** a new **Celestial Body** always gives you, without CE, what Reinforcement gives a sorcerer (speed, damage, jump and defense), driven by your Physical. Physical can go up to **300**; you only use Physical, which **rises on its own** (+2 per level, +3 per grade; the other stats, Luck included, are locked and their scrolls and relics give Physical). Your body **regenerates**, **resists** harmful effects and knockback, and **X** now lets you smell and hear what is around you (people in white, everything else in red) without CE. The *Keen Senses* Art is gone.
- **Tool pouch:** more slots as you rank up (3 → 7; Heavenly Restriction 5 → 9).
- **Uzumaki** shows its damage with 10 Grade 1 curses too, not only with 10 Grade 3.
- **More curses everywhere:** Grade 4 and 3 curses now spawn 50 % more often, show up more in daylight, and more of them can gather nearby (day and night). Crawling Curses and Grade 4 Cursed Spirits come in bigger groups, and pack curses sometimes spawn as a whole nest of their kind.
- **Cursed biomes are full of curses:** the new biomes now spawn their own curses much more often. The Cursed Wasteland spawns every curse three times as often, even in daylight; the Residue Marsh is full of water curses (with a rare Eso or Kechizu), the Cedar Forest of moths, umbrellas and hanged curses (rarely Tamamo-no-Mae), the Primeval Forest of roaches, centipedes and Ko-Guy (rarely Kurourushi), the Volcanic Slopes of Crushing Hulks and Great Cursed Birds (rarely Akuro-o) and the Disaster Reef of drowned curses and squids. More curses can gather in these biomes at once.
- **CE Tanks work together:** tanks that touch form one storage of up to 27 (a 3 × 3 × 3 cube), filling from the bottom up. Each tank shows how full it is in 5 steps and glows brighter the fuller it gets, a comparator reads the whole group, holding right-click keeps pouring your CE in, and breaking a tank moves its energy into the rest of the group instead of losing it. Puppets and the Puppeteer's Table draw from the group (Mega Mechamaru takes 1000 CE from it).
- **Exorcism Record:** every type of curse you exorcise (or help exorcise, or absorb with Curse Manipulation) is written into your Record, with a chat notice the first time. Curse gestation will only let you grow types from your Record. Operators: `/ce exorcism`.
- **Cursed Residue:** what's left after exorcising a curse. Grade 4 and 3 curses drop it (Grade 2 sometimes). It's the material for curse gestation.
- **Curse gestation:** every curse type you exorcise goes into your Exorcism Record. Build a **Gestation Altar** (3 Cursed Residue, 2 amethyst shards, a CE Tank and 3 polished deepslate), pick a curse you know, and it takes the CE from the tanks within 8 blocks plus the materials: a Grade 4 curse costs 3000 CE and 6 residues and takes 20 minutes, a Grade 3 one costs 6000 CE, 10 residues and a Soul Core and takes 40. Time runs with the world clock, even if you're away. You get a **Curse Orb**: break it and the curse becomes your companion (one at a time); sneak + right-click your companion to turn it back into an orb. Geto skips the wait, pays 40 % less CE and can gestate up to Grade 1.
- **New looks for gestation:** CE Tanks have a window showing the blue CE inside, swirling and bubbling, at 5 fill levels, and tanks placed together look like one big tank (one copper frame around the group, one continuous window); the Gestation Altar has its own model (deepslate pedestal, copper-banded pillar, ritual cradle and amethyst crystals); the Curse Orb is a 3D black sphere with a purple swirl, and Cursed Residue has its own icon.
- **Weapon fixes:** Mei Mei's axe and Megumi's Black Sword are now held with the edge facing down instead of up, the Split Soul Katana no longer turns the wrong way when held in the off hand, and Mai's revolver icon in the inventory now points the right way and fits its slot.
- **More colourful curses:** several curses that looked washed-out grey now have their own muted colour (the Crawling Curse, the Drain Hand, the Ceiling Spider, the Crushing Mass, the Hanged Curse and the Grade 4, 3 and 2 Cursed Spirits, each grade a different colour).
- **New models for three canon curses:** the Rainbow Dragon is now a four-legged cream oriental dragon with antlers, mane and whiskers; Eso has his muscular look with the chest band, suspenders and bow tie; the Finger Bearer is a big, muscular, pale blue half-human with dark markings on his head, red claws and a black loincloth.
- **The Smallpox Deity** has its canon look: a floating hunched mass with a huge mane, an upside-down grinning face with horns, pockmarked grey skin and a single coiled tail.
- **Three more canon curses redone:** Toji's Inventory Curse is a muted purple worm of round, bead-like segments; the Shoulder Curse is a small, thin black creature with two eyes on stalks and a huge grin; and the Hostage Curse is now an enormous blue mass with two pairs of arms, an eye on each side and a mouth big enough to swallow people whole.
- **Tamamo-no-Mae is no longer a fox:** she now matches the anime: a tall figure in a floor-length coral kimono with a white polka-dot front panel, striped light-green trims, a dark green ribbon, wide sleeves, a white mask with slit eyes and a toothy grin, and long black hair. Her aura is coral instead of gold.
- **Cursed Centipede and Ko-Guy redone from the anime:** the centipedes are now Geto's red centipedes from Jujutsu Kaisen 0, rearing up with a purple-lipped human grin; Ko-Guy is a hunched, muscular khaki humanoid with a grasshopper head, a spotted green shell and long lilac wings.
- **Kurourushi has two forms:** at rest it hibernates as a tall black shroud with a red cockroach face and long antennae; when it fights it becomes a red humanoid cockroach with four arms and a torn black cape. Its Cursed Cockroaches are now dark, winged and slightly smaller, glowing purple with cursed energy.
- **Kechizu redone from the anime:** a hunched, blue-green humanoid with a hump, an eyeless head with a small mouth of teeth, and a second, enormous bleeding grin on its chest. It is now a little shorter than Eso.
- **Four more curses redone from the series:** the Cursed Squids are now pale blue-white, glow in the dark and fly mantle-first; the Cursed Manta is a huge pink ray with two big eyes and is now Grade 3; Akuro-o is a giant red oni with four eyes, three black horns and an axe in each hand; Ganesha has pinkish-grey skin, red eyes, a star on its forehead and a spotted black vest.
- **Fly Heads and the giant school curse redone:** Fly Heads are small winged pink heads with dangling arms, in three different faces; the Giant Curse of the School is now a six-legged, grey-green beast with an ochre mane, red eyes and a huge mouth of flat teeth.
- **Curse domain decorations touched up:** the cockroach piles in Kurourushi's domain now show proper cockroaches, and the jade elephant idol in Ganesha's temple has Ganesha's new face.
- **Models for the new canon curses:** the Cursed Womb of the Eishu Detention Center is a glowing amber sphere with a curled-up fetus inside; Ubusunagami is an earth god of cracked clay and moss with a wooden mask and a sacred rope; Geto's semi-Grade 1 curse is a pale, furry creature that floats cross-legged with a long drooping nose and red eyes; and a giant grey one-eyed curse wears a necklace of heads.
- **Special Grade curses get their own domains:** the Smallpox Deity's **Graveyard** (its coffin becomes a sure-hit that even Infinity can't stop, and it traps you again after you escape), Kurourushi's **Plague Nest** (more cockroaches climb on you every second, each one adding damage), Tamamo-no-Mae's **Shrine of the Nine Foxes** (fox fire, nausea and an illusion that turns you around), Akuro-o's **Oni Fortress** (earthquakes that lift you and slam you down) and Ganesha's **Temple of Obstacles**, an open domain with no barrier that keeps launching everything that isn't huge into the sky. The generic Special Grade curse keeps the Cursed Womb.
- **Canon curses in their places:** the **Eishū Reformatory** is now guarded by the **Cursed Womb**; exorcise it within 3 minutes or it hatches into a Special Grade **Finger Bearer**. The **Ubusunagami** sometimes haunts roadside shrines, and the giant **Head-Necklace Curse** can show up in cursed cemeteries.
- **Curse domains are decorated inside:** rows of gravestones, sotoba tablets and shrouded corpses in the Graveyard; cockroach piles and rusty pipes in the Plague Nest; nine red torii, fox-fire lanterns and fox statues in the Shrine of the Nine Foxes; boulders, stalagmites and bone heaps in the Oni Fortress. Ganesha's Temple of Obstacles now raises a small temple with an elephant idol behind him: **break it and the domain collapses**. All the new pieces are also creative-only building blocks.
- **Exorcism Record in K:** a new **Record** tab lists every curse by grade, with the ones you know by name (marked if you can gestate them) and the rest as «???». The Gestation Altar now shows a progress view while it gestates (the orb, a progress bar and the time left, updated every second); click the orb when it's ready to take it.
- **Heavenly Restriction (energy) gets 7 new Body Arts (no CE):** **No Presence** (passive: crouch still and enemies lose you; your first hit deals double), **Throw Weapon** (comes back if you hold the Infinite Chain in your other hand, otherwise it stays where it lands), **Afterimages** (a dash that hits everyone in your path and leaves a decoy), **Deflect** (send projectiles back and parry a hit with a counter; with the Inverted Spear of Heaven, technique projectiles too, and they keep their effect: a deflected Red explodes on its own caster), **Hunter's Mark** (+15% damage and a trail only you can see), **Wind Strike** (a punch that sends a pressure wave) and **Awakening** (20 s at your limit, then fatigue). Superhuman Dodge now goes farther the more Physique you have. **Curse Camouflage:** with the Inventory Worm in your other hand, hit Fly Heads to store them (up to 30), then release them to fight beside you and draw enemies away; survivors return to the worm. The energy Heavenly Restriction can now walk through Veils and takes no fall damage.
- **Toji and Maki use the new Body Arts too:** they throw a copy of their weapon from range (never point-blank), dash through you with Afterimages, parry with Deflect and fire Wind Strikes. They now react to what's coming at them: arrows and, when Toji holds the Inverted Spear, your Blue or Red orbs can be sent straight back at you (about half the time, at most once every 15 seconds). A deflected orb turns to face its new direction.
- **Geto swallows Curse Orbs:** as Curse Manipulation, breaking a Curse Orb puts that curse in your storage (and in your Exorcism Record) instead of making it your companion.
- **Companion curse** (ready for the Curse Orb): a Grade 4 or 3 curse that follows you and fights what you attack and what attacks you, using its own moves. It grows with you like a summon, needs no cursed energy to keep, heals slowly out of combat and keeps its tamed temperament (a coward hides behind you when hurt, a glutton devours what falls near it). Only one at a time; it leaves and comes back with you when you log out, follows you between dimensions and waits for you if you die. If it dies, it's lost.

- **Six new biomes where curses are born** (curses are born from human fear; each biome is a fear). They appear in new chunks, inside vanilla biomes:
  - **Cursed Wasteland:** grey-violet grass, dead trees, ash and dark fog, rare inside plains, forests and taigas.
  - **Residue Marsh:** black-green water and mud in the heart of swamps and mangroves.
  - **Cedar Forest:** tall Japanese cedars, ferns and light mist, like the mountains around the Jujutsu school.
  - **Primeval Forest:** giant trees, moss and spores deep in jungles.
  - **Volcanic Slopes:** basalt, blackstone, tuff, basalt columns, lava deltas and falling ash.
  - **Disaster Reef:** dead coral and dark water inside warm oceans.
- **Curse Nests:** small abandoned buildings (a wooden house, a stone warehouse or a ruined roadside shrine) with a chest and a **Residue Core** inside. The core keeps spawning curses while a player is near, until you break it (it drops Cursed Residue). Nests get stronger the farther they are from world spawn (Grade 4 up to Grade 1) and are much more common, and one grade stronger, in the Cursed Wasteland and the Residue Marsh.
- **Seven new places full of curses:**
  - **Roadside shrines** in cedar and other forests, with a small torii and an offering chest (sometimes a Seal Talisman).
  - **Sugisawa High School:** curses inside at night, and on the roof the giant curse drawn by a sealed Sukuna's finger (5% chance in the rooftop chest).
  - **Abandoned hospitals:** the urban curses (Slit-Mouthed Woman, Shoulder Curse, Hostage Curse, Floating Eye).
  - **Cursed cemeteries:** graves, a mausoleum, hanged curses and moths, guarded by a **Gashadokuro**.
  - **Yasohachi Bridge** over rivers, guarded by **Eso** (who brings his brother Kechizu).
  - **Eishu Reformatory:** rows of identical cells guarded by the **Finger Bearer**, with a guaranteed Sukuna's finger.
  - **Sealing altars:** a pillar wrapped in talismans with a Sukuna's finger on top; Grade 3-2 curses gather around it.
- **Special Grade places:**
  - **Nine Foxes Shrine** in cedar forests and cherry groves: a path of red torii, fox-fire lanterns and a hall guarded by **Tamamo-no-Mae**.
  - **Disaster lairs**, where the four Disaster Curses can now awaken besides their old spots: **Jogo's crater** in the Volcanic Slopes, **Hanami's hollow tree** in the Primeval Forest, **Dagon's sunken ruin** in the Disaster Reef and **Mahito's sewers** underground (at any time of day).
- **Maki Zen'in now lives in the world:** she can appear during the day near Special Grade players (instead of Gojo, sometimes), and she guards her own structure, the **Zen'in Clan Estate**: a walled compound with a main hall, a raked gravel courtyard, a pond, a training ground and the clan's **armory** (with a chest of cursed tools). Wake her at the altar as a Special Grade sorcerer; beat her for a guardian chest full of Zen'in weapons.
### ⚖️ Balance
- **Grade 4 and Grade 3 curses:** no longer have damage toughness (that is for Grade 2 and up), flying ones stay at most 4 blocks above the ground (but still climb to reach you if you stand on something tall), and what they shoot or cast from afar (spit, gaze, tongue, ink jet) comes at most every 10 seconds.
- **Heavenly Restriction (energy) early game:** born with 30 Physical (was 60) and the **Celestial Body grows with your grade** (×0.4 ungraded … ×1.2 at Special Grade). Before, with a cursed tool it could kill a Special Grade curse at level 1.
- **Easier first minutes:** curses no longer get the extra low-grade toughness meant for fights between sorcerers, and the generic Grade 4 curse has 45 health (was 66). A new player now needs about 15 reinforced punches (7 with a reinforced stone sword) instead of 46.
- **Boss altars wake up on right-click:** the guardian of a boss structure no longer appears just because a Special Grade sorcerer walks close; right-click the altar when you're ready. The altar's message shows once when you enter its area and once when you leave, instead of repeating every few seconds.
- **Structure guardians wake up for the right sorcerer:** curses that guard a structure (like Tamamo-no-Mae in the Nine Foxes Shrine) only appear when a player of a high enough grade is nearby, using the same rule as natural curse spawns. A newcomer can explore a Special Grade's shrine without waking it.
- **Heavenly Restriction Power** now matches a balanced sorcerer of your level and grade (it used to be your Physical, which let you one-shot players of your level). *Awakening* gives +25 Power instead.
- **Grade-based swarms:** the extra curses that join a swarm because of your grade give half EXP and drop no Cursed Residue.
- **Curses:** the Hostage Curse is tougher and slower to match its size; the Cursed Ray has Grade 3 stats; the head-necklace giant is now about 6.5 blocks tall and much tougher.
- **Potential Vessels are born with cursed energy** (like a Normal sorcerer) but no technique: you can use Reinforcement, Sensing, Guard and the Jujutsu Arts from the start and hurt stronger curses while you look for a host. Your host still brings its technique, and your CE base becomes the higher of yours and the host's. The Severance Seal now leaves you with your own (empty) energy instead of none.
- **Host relics are equally likely:** wherever they drop at random (vanilla chests, Special Grade curses, Disaster Curses, the Nine Foxes Shrine), a Sukuna Finger, Ryu's Comb and the other reincarnated sorcerers' relics now share the same chance, so every host technique is possible. Sukuna's places still give fingers, and the **Sugisawa School rooftop chest always has one** (like episode 1).
- **Overwhelming power one-shots weak curses:** when you are two grades or more above a curse or NPC (50 Power), your hits are no longer capped to a fraction of its health. A Special Grade sorcerer now exorcises Grade 4 to Grade 2 curses in one blow (before, even a fully charged Dismantle stopped at 92 % of a Grade 4's health). Players and bosses keep the usual per-hit limit.
- **Summons with a lot of health are no longer nearly indestructible:** they keep only part of your toughness the more health they have (Mahoraga, Agito, Max Elephant…), so strong techniques like Hollow Purple take them down in a couple of hits (Mahoraga from a Special Grade owner: about 3 Hollow Purples). Summons with 200 health or less keep their full toughness. Rika follows the same rule: still the toughest summon, but no longer near-immortal. So do Geto's released curses and your curse companion (a Special Grade curse released by a Special Grade Geto now falls to about 3 Hollow Purples instead of 7). Ritual shikigami are unchanged.
- **Mahito's 0.2-second domain** (Shift + Self-Embodiment of Perfection) now has its own 60 s cooldown and costs half, like Gojo's 0.2-second Unlimited Void (it used to spend the whole domain's 2-minute cooldown and full cost). It leaves the technique burnt out for 5 s.
- **Full adaptation also blocks a technique's effects:** a fully adapted Mahoraga, or a player whose Mahoraga's Wheel is fully turned against a technique, only takes its (reduced) damage: no more pull from Blue or push from Red.
- **Rika in phase 2 is calmer:** she follows Yuta and only attacks whoever he attacks, whoever attacks him and nearby curses (no more chasing animals or other mobs).
- **Mahoraga's Wheel adapts to Infinity:** every hit Infinity stops turns your wheel; after 3 turns your hits, techniques and their effects go through it.

### 🐛 Fixes
- **Heavenly Restriction (energy) stays traitless:** the Six Eyes item no longer opens for a body without cursed energy (it is not used up), and the Profile describes the current Restriction (Physical that rises on its own, crossing Veils and all its Body Arts). A Potential Vessel's latent trait now says it awakens when you receive your host.
- **Big curses no longer vanish at the edge of the screen:** wide models (the Head Necklace giant, the Rainbow Dragon, the centipede, the manta ray…) used to pop out of view while part of them was still on screen.
- **The screen no longer stretches when you move very fast:** the speed field-of-view effect is now capped for everyone (Heavenly Restriction, speed techniques, Speed effects…), not only with Reinforcement. It never widens more than plain sprinting does; bow zoom is unchanged.
- **Tool pouch** in the off hand no longer opens on every right-click and gets in the way of your techniques: it only swaps weapons while sneaking. Weapons left in slots you can no longer use can be taken out.
- `/ce origin heavenly` now gives the Heavenly Restriction aptitudes and starting Physical.
- **Fewer Spanish texts with the game in English:** the Gestation Altar menu, Geto's menu, the ability help, stat tooltips and a few chat messages are now translated.
- **Less chat spam when you first join a world:** instead of ~16 lines, you now get 3-5 short ones (your origin, the starter kit and your first mission) plus an on-screen notice; the full breakdown of your origin, aptitudes, species and traits is in K → Profile. Some of those lines were not translated to English and now are.
- **Cursed Spirit Manipulation:** Crush no longer stuns through Infinity, and it now damages targets that were already stunned. The Smallpox Deity's coffin is stopped by Infinity. Night Parade of a Hundred Demons curses and the cockroaches from Kurourushi's Plague no longer count toward your summon limit (Release was blocked while they were out). Uzumaki now shows its damage on the K screen.
- **Cursed Spirit Manipulation, quality of life:** Geto's menu lets you choose whether Uzumaki takes your weakest curses (default) or your strongest. Abilities that need a specific curse (Squid Shot, Crush, Flying Curse, Rainbow Dragon, Kurourushi, Smallpox Deity) only show up in your list when you have that curse stored. Disaster curses (Jogo, Hanami, Dagon, Mahito) can now be broken down with Uzumaki into an Extracted Technique, as Kenjaku does with Mahito. Curses you have out now count toward Pandemonium's damage. NPC Getos absorb weakened curses nearby and recall allies before they get exorcised. "Dios Viruela" is now "Dios de la Viruela" in Spanish.
- **Cursed Spirit Manipulation, stronger:** released curses now grow with your power like shikigami; your storage has no limit and there is no limit on curses out; crouch + Release releases a batch (1 + your grade, cooldown 10 s) as many times as you want; a nearby curse shields you, taking 40% of each hit; curses rest less the higher your grade. Squid Shot now launches the Cursed Squid itself, which bursts into blinding ink on impact, instead of a beam of light. Absorb now also recalls: look at one of your curses to send it back to storage, or crouch + Absorb to recall them all; Geto's menu opens with crouch + Absorb when you have no curses out (Absorb no longer pops it open when you miss a curse).
- Max Elephant's Flood (the player's partial manifestation) now describes itself correctly: a thin straight jet of water, not a cone.
- Flying Curse: the manta ray was drawn more than four times too big, with the rider inside it and the camera blocked; it is now its proper size and you sit on its back.

## 1.6.0 — The Arsenal Update

### ✨ New
- **Ten Shadows overhaul (feedback):**
  - Fusions no longer take slots in your ability list: use **Shift + ability key on the base shikigami** (Divine Dogs → Totality, Toad → Winged Toads, Nue → Giant Nue, Tiger Funeral or Round Deer → Agito).
  - **Mahoraga** no longer burns out your technique, inherits your Mahoraga's Wheel adaptations when summoned (the wheel can't be used while he's out), moves faster, smashes through blocks on the way to his target and, once fully adapted to an enemy domain, breaks it.
  - Shikigami summoned before your domain stay out when your technique burns out (you just can't summon more until it ends); they run to catch up with you and slip back through the shadows if they get stuck.
  - Giant Nue has a configurable size and its lightning breaks leaves, glass and other light blocks. Max Elephant aims its flood at its target; your Elephant Flood is now a thin, long jet like Piercing Blood.
- **A calmer HUD in combat:**
  - **Status icons:** every active state (Reinforcement, RCT, Sensing, Guard, Simple Domain, the Zone, technique toggles…) now has its own icon next to its name. Prefer less text? Switch K → Settings → States to **Icons** and everyday states become small icon chips with a thin timer bar (critical ones like Burnout or an enemy domain always stay as full cards); the same page shows what each icon means.
  - **Settings screen redone:** four pages (HUD · Target · States · Effects) with one setting per row, its name on the left and its value on the button, so nothing overflows any more.
  - **New automatic HUD mode (default, H to change):** the full block shows while you're fighting or using abilities and shrinks to the thin CE bar after 5 quiet seconds with full CE. Your level and EXP line only appears for a moment when it changes.
  - **Action bar messages no longer trample each other:** each stays readable for at least a second, extra ones wait their turn, and red warnings always go first.
- **Cleaner HUD, nothing under your crosshair:** what you know about the one you're looking at — the Six Eyes panel or your Sensing read — now sits in one small target card in a corner (top left by default; change it in K → Settings → Target). Choose how much the Six Eyes tell you in K → Settings: Brief (name, health and CE), Normal (+ what they're doing) or Full (+ who they are and their stats). The Sensing read is shown as bars and pips in the target's energy colour. Vows and your current mission no longer take up space in the combat HUD: find them in K (Vows and Hunt tabs).
- **Mega Mechamaru: Absolute Mode.** The giant puppet now stands 15 blocks tall (configurable) and you can pilot it from its head: right-click to climb in, move with WASD where you look, the ability key punches and Shift + ability key fires the Ultimate Cannon. It takes the damage aimed at you.
- **New mod icon:** the cursed seal 呪 in an aura of cursed energy, drawn in the mod's ink style (replaces the placeholder in the mod list and Mod Menu).
- **Every ability now has its own icon:** the remaining 21 techniques (Angel, Auspicious Beasts, Brain Transplant, Comedian, Construction, Cursed Corpse, Crow Manipulation, Cursed Speech, Cursed Spirit Manipulation, Granite Blast, Idle Death Gamble, Ice Formation, Judgeman, Kenjaku, Disaster Plants, Projection, Sky Manipulation, Star Rage, Love Rendezvous, Straw Doll and Disaster Tides) join the 10 that already had them — 142 new icons, including Construction's Liquid Metal Shield and Insect Armor and Mahito's new forms. Only the Arts keep the shared emblem.

While G is held the wheel doesn't change your hotbar slot, and the HUD shows three abilities on each side of the selected one. A quick tap of G still selects the next ability (Shift + G, the previous).
  - **Hold Z to use your Arts for a moment:** a quick tap still switches lists, but if you hold Z the other list is active only while it's held — Z + V casts an Art without leaving your technique. Letting go switches back without cancelling what you cast; a held Art or a charge keeps going.

### ⚖️ Balance
- **Common power scale.** Players, NPCs, curses, Disaster Curses, summons and bosses follow the same rules. Everyone has a Power level, and damage taken is divided by a toughness that grows with Power, so fights between equals last the same no matter who fights whom, while grade gaps stay decisive (+30% damage per grade above your target).
- **Per-hit caps** between equals: 20% of max health for normal hits and abilities, 30% for Black Flash, 60% for Maximum Techniques (×1.4 damage; two of them decide a fight) and 75% for Hollow Purple: Blast.
- **Reverse Cursed Technique:** players heal up to 4% of their max health per second (at RCT 100), and from RCT 60 turning it on instantly heals 15%. But it is now **expensive**: 8 CE per health point actually healed. Tanking a fight or a domain with RCT drains your cursed energy.
- **More health from Physical:** up to 150 max health for a fully trained sorcerer.
- **Domains** kill an equal player in about 20-25 s if nobody counters them (sure-hit damage raised across the board). Domain fatigue: each Domain Expansion within 5 minutes makes the next one 25% more expensive and its Burnout 50% longer.
- **Melee:** cursed tools add 0.1 × Reinforcement. Weapon affinity adds +0.25 to the strike, and a soft cap makes multipliers past ×4 count half, so stacked strikes no longer reach thousands of damage. Ratio's strike is a heavy hit (35 CE, 4 s cooldown).
- **Maximum Techniques:** Granite Overflow, Supersonic Speed and both Resonances deal less, so Hollow Purple and Fuga stay on top. Hollow Purple: Blast is stronger than the normal Purple but has a longer cooldown.
- **Cooldowns:** Ember Insects 3.5 s, Supernova 7 s, Granite Pellets and Granite Blast 5 s, Piercing Blood 5 s, Eruption 7 s, Rika's Arm 3 s, Dismantle and the Gavel 3 s.
- **Buffs:** Max Elephant (5 per wave, 15 s), Constructed Bullet (30 damage, 20 s), Clap Barrage (6 per hit, 8 s).
- **Technique Restoration** no longer resets the Domain and Maximum Technique cooldowns (level II halves what's left; 60 s cooldown).
- **Domain barriers are sturdier:** 1500 health at Barrier 100 (was 1000). Terrain-destroying techniques can take at most 20% of a barrier (or the Malevolent Shrine's shrine) per second, and hits deal 60% of their damage to it, so breaking out takes real effort.
- **Flying NPCs** (Limitless, Gojo) fly in bursts of 6 s and then land for 12 s, so fighters without flight can reach them.
- Stunned and asleep mobs can't attack or use curse abilities. Brain damage and confiscation block curse abilities too (except Simple Domain).

### 🔧 Changes
- **Reinforcement:** walking, sprinting, swimming and jumping no longer drain hunger or saturation while it's active.
- **Collapse (7:3)** now breaks the ground and walls around you.
- **Output** no longer counts twice in technique damage. Damage was rebalanced: similar at high Output, a bit lower at low Output.
- **Stunned and Asleep** now also stop attacks and technique use, which includes Cursed Speech's Stop and Sleep.
- **Barrier stat:** now also lowers domain upkeep, raises barrier health, decides domain clashes and gives resistance inside enemy domains.
- **Divine Dogs** now follow and defend non-player owners too.
- **Infinity** now stops **100%** of damage with a source, explosions included: nothing touches you. Mastery lowers the CE cost per blocked point (2 at Mastery 1, 1.1 at Mastery 10). If you can't pay for a hit, Infinity collapses and that hit lands. It also pushes away anything that gets close harder.
- **Cursed tools** now scale with **Reinforcement** (was Output) and deal their bonus inside the same hit, so technique multipliers and Black Flash boost it too.

- **Reverse Cursed Technique: Restore Technique** now also **resets all your ability cooldowns**, including Domain Expansion and Maximum Techniques. It now requires RCT 60 and Control 40.
- **Restore Technique II** (new, RCT 100 · Control 80 · Special Grade): no cooldown, but every use risks **Brain Damage**. The risk grows if you chain restorations and shrinks with Control.
- **Brain Damage** (new effect): level I blocks your innate technique and Domain Expansion; level II also leaves you unable to move. Active RCT heals it twice as fast. **Unlimited Void** now inflicts Brain Damage II on everyone inside.

- **Burnout** now comes in two kinds. After a Maximum Technique or a Domain Expansion you only get **Technique Burnout**: your innate technique is blocked, but Reinforcement, CE Guard, RCT, Arts and CE regeneration keep working. Running out of CE still causes a full Burnout.

- **Chant** now shows a warning while you recite, not only when you release: your progress on the action bar, a chat warning for players within 24 blocks when you start and when you finish, and runes around you that everyone can see.

- **Pop-up notices** when you level up, rank up your grade, raise your Mastery, unlock an ability or awaken your technique.
- **New Settings tab** in the Sorcerer Screen (K): HUD corner, size and offset; status card side and size; turn notices, the Black Flash effect and ability icons on or off. Saved in `config/jjk-client.json`.
- **Ability icons** in the HUD and the Technique tab.
- **Black Flash screen effect:** a black screen with red lightning, a red flash and the 黒閃 title.

- **Big power rebalance:** all technique damage now gets a **technique power** multiplier from Output (×3 at Output 100 and ×4 at 200 for players; ×1.5 for NPCs and curses). Physical now gives up to +80 health and +100 % damage; Reinforcement up to +250 % damage and −70 % damage taken. Blue base damage 5 → 7.
- **Hollow Purple** now starts the tunnel right at your hands (no stone layer left at the entrance). **Hollow Purple: Blast** explodes in a much bigger sphere: radius 10 + 8 per charge.

- **Six Eyes panel:** with the Six Eyes trait, the HUD shows what you're looking at: health, remaining CE, technique and Mastery, what they're using (Reinforcement, RCT, Guard, toggles, what they're charging), their state (domain, Burnout, the Zone, confiscation…) and main stats. Can be hidden in Settings.

- **Config file `config/jjk.json`:** every balance value of the mod (~830) can now be changed without recompiling, plus quick multipliers (all technique damage, CE costs, cooldowns, damage taken by players, per technique and per ability). `/ce config reload` applies most changes instantly.

- **In-game balance screen:** Sorcerer Screen (K) → Settings → *Mod balance*, or Mod Menu's *Configure* button (Mod Menu is optional). Browse by section or search every value, see what differs from the default, save and apply instantly.
- **Server balance sync:** clients now receive the server's `config/jjk.json`, so the Sorcerer Screen shows the server's numbers.

- **Disaster curses in the world:** they now appear on their own near players of Grade 2 or higher. Jogo guards **ruined portals**, Hanami **jungle temples**, Mahito **swamp huts** at night and Dagon roams **warm ocean reefs**. Each site spawns at most one every 5 in-game days.
- **Boss loot and combat lines:** disaster curses drop Cursed Wombs, a chance of a Sukuna Finger or a Ryu Comb, themed items (blaze rods, a heart of the sea, a totem…) and 500 XP, and speak when they appear, at half health, when expanding their domain and when they die.

Configurable (`AREA_SCALE_MIN/MAX`). The same applies to every energy-release ability of the other techniques (Dismantle, Spiderweb, Fuga, World Slash, Collapse, Piercing Blood, Supernova, Pure Love Beam, Blast Away, Sleep, Uzumaki, the Disaster techniques…); melee strikes, weapons, single-target commands, shikigami and domains are unchanged.

Disaster curses and Grade 1 curses could previously ignore Malevolent Shrine indefinitely.
